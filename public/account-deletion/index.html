Delete your Solray account

Operated by Bobby ehf., Iceland.

Last updated: May 16, 2026

You can delete your Solray account and the personal data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do this.

1. Inside the Solray app

If you have the Solray app on your phone or are signed in on the web:

  • Open Solray and sign in.
  • Tap your profile icon, then tap the settings gear.
  • Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  • Confirm when prompted.

Your account is removed within minutes. You will be signed out everywhere.

2. By email

If you cannot access the app, email privacy@solray.ai from the address on your Solray account. Include the words Delete my account in the subject line.

We confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days. We may ask one question to verify it is you before deleting.

What gets deleted

When you delete your account, we permanently remove:

  • Your name, email, and password.
  • Your birth date, time, and place (used to generate your chart).
  • Your natal chart, Human Design, and Gene Keys data.
  • Your Higher Self chat history and any memory we built from your conversations.
  • Your soul connections and compatibility data.
  • Any photos or images you uploaded.
  • Push notification tokens and device identifiers.

What we keep, and why

We retain a small amount of data when required by law or to protect you and us:

  • Billing records. Payment receipts, invoice numbers, and amounts paid are kept for 7 years as required by Iceland tax law. These do not contain card numbers; payment cards are stored only by our processor (Teya or Google Play), never by us.
  • Abuse and fraud signals. A minimal record (hashed email, account ID, reason) is kept for up to 12 months if your account was suspended for abuse, fraud, or breach of terms. This prevents the same actor from re-creating an account.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying metrics. Anonymized counts (e.g., 23 users in May) do not contain personal data and are not deleted.

Subscription cancellation

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel a recurring subscription bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. To stop future charges:

  • Google Play: open the Play Store app, tap your profile, choose Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, find Solray, then Cancel.
  • Apple App Store: open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, find Solray, then Cancel.
  • Web (Teya): contact support@solray.ai and we will cancel and confirm.

Children

Solray is not for users under 16. If a parent or guardian believes a child has created an account, email privacy@solray.ai and we will delete it immediately.

Questions

For anything about this page, your data, or the deletion process, email privacy@solray.ai.
