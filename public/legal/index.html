3. Privacy Policy

3.1 Who is the controller

Bobby ehf. (Iceland) is the data controller of personal data processed through the Service. Contact: privacy@solray.ai.

3.2 What we collect

Account information: name, email address, password (stored only as a salted bcrypt hash), and timestamps.

Birth data: birth date, birth time, birth city, latitude and longitude. Used to compute your chart. Treated as sensitive; stored encrypted at rest in our managed Postgres database; never shared with third parties.

Profile content: optional profile photo, display preferences, public-profile flag.

Chat content: the messages you send to the Oracle and the Oracle's replies. Stored to give the Oracle continuity across sessions and to let you re-read past conversations.

Memory: the Oracle synthesises short, distilled memories from your conversations (one to two sentences each, not raw chat) so future sessions feel continuous. You can view and delete these at any time from your profile.

Payment metadata: a Teya subscription identifier, billing status, current plan. We do not see or store card numbers.

Device and usage: standard server logs (IP, user-agent, request timestamps), retained for security and operations for 30 days. Optional product analytics (pages visited, features used) which you may opt out of from your profile.

Voice transcripts: if you use the voice composer, your audio is sent to a transcription provider (Groq Whisper or OpenAI Whisper) for speech-to-text. The audio file is not retained; the resulting text becomes part of your chat history.

3.3 What we do not collect

We do not collect your contacts, your photo library, your precise location, your microphone outside of the explicit voice composer, or your camera. We do not run third-party advertising trackers. We do not sell your data.

3.4 Why we process this data (legal bases under GDPR Article 6)

  • Performance of contract for the core service (chart calculation, Oracle chat, billing).
  • Consent for the optional collective layer (Akashic Record, see Section 4) and for any non-essential analytics or research.
  • Legitimate interest for security, fraud prevention, and operational logs (30-day retention).
  • Legal obligation for tax records and lawful requests by competent authorities.

3.5 Profiling and automated decision-making

The Oracle generates personalised text using your chart and chat history. This is reflective content, not legally significant automated decision-making under GDPR Article 22 (it does not produce legal or similarly significant effects). You always retain the right to disregard the Oracle's output.

4. The Akashic Record (collective layer)

Solray operates a collective-intelligence layer called the Akashic Record. When users opt in, anonymised pattern signals derived from their charts are aggregated to help the Oracle recognise patterns across the field. We never share another user's identity, content, or memory with you.

4.1 What enters the Akashic Record

  • Coarse chart components only (e.g., sun sign, Human Design type, Gene Key spheres). Never raw birth data.
  • Aggregate pattern signals computed from your components, not from your chats.
  • Themes are synthesised aggregate claims, not memory excerpts; raw chat content does not enter the collective layer.

4.2 K-anonymity floor

Cohorts and patterns surface in the Oracle's responses only when they involve at least 10 distinct users (k ≥ 10). Smaller groupings are suppressed.

4.3 Append-only history

Chart-component changes (for example, when you correct a birth time) are recorded as immutable events alongside the current state, so collective patterns can be traced over time. These events are deleted when you delete your account.

5. Tiered consent

You can grant or revoke each of the following scopes independently from your profile. They are not bundled, and revoking one does not affect the others.

  • Private Oracle use. Implicit when you sign up. Required for the Service to function.
  • Personalisation memory. Lets the Oracle remember context across your sessions.
  • Anonymous product analytics. Aggregate usage statistics that help us improve the product.
  • Anonymous cohort learning. Your coarse chart components contribute to the Akashic Record.
  • Anonymous retrieval training. Anonymised patterns may inform future Oracle improvements.
  • Public Oracle quote eligible. Marks a future possibility (not active today): if we ever post anonymised aggregate observations on a public surface, your contribution may be eligible. Default OFF.
  • Research or protocol experiments. Marks eligibility for future opt-in research collaborations. Default OFF.
  • Governance participation eligible. Marks eligibility for future opt-in governance experiments. Default OFF. Eligibility is not participation; any actual participation requires per-event opt-in.

Each consent grant is timestamped, version-tracked, and stored alongside its scope. You can revoke any grant at any time. Revocation takes effect immediately for ongoing processing; aggregate statistics that have already left an identifiable form are retained per Section 9.
